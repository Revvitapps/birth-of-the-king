Thank you for your purchase! Everything you need to teach all seven lessons is in this download. Here is what each folder holds and how to open it.

What is inside

1
TEACHER EDITION (one PDF, 110 pages)
The book you teach from: seven lesson plans, 25 readings, paintings and maps, the student pages and the answer key. Opens in any PDF reader on a computer, tablet or phone.
2
FLIP BOOK (for a computer or TV screen)
The same 110 pages as a book you page through on screen, with a lesson menu and a full screen button.
  1. Open the folder named 2 Flip Book.
  2. Double-click the file index.html. It opens in your web browser.
  3. Click a page or use the arrow keys to turn pages. Click Full screen or press F to fill the screen.
Keep index.html and the pages folder together. No internet is needed.
3
STUDENT PAGES BY LESSON (seven PDFs)
The coloring pages and worksheets for each lesson in their own file, so you print only what you need each week.
4
STUDENT WORKBOOK (one PDF, 40 pages)
All 38 student pages in a single file with a cover, for printing a full workbook for each child.

Printing

Print on standard 8.5 × 11 paper and choose “Fit to page.” The coloring and activity pages print well in black and white.
